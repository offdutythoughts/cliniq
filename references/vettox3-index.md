## Scope note vs `vetgirl-tox-notes.md`

The two toxicology references do **not** overlap cleanly:

- `vetgirl-tox-notes.md` (ASPCA APCC 2023) is **small-animal, clinical, current** — toxic
  dose thresholds per kg, decontamination decision rules, antidote dosing. It stays the
  first-line reference for `DIS-TOX-*` work.
- `vettox3.md` (2018) is **mechanistic and multi-species**, heavy on production animals,
  wildlife, and regulatory/analytical material. Use it for pathophysiology, toxicokinetics,
  target-organ mechanism, plant/mycotoxin/zootoxin breadth, and for the toxicants VETgirl
  does not cover.

Where the two disagree on a dose, **VETgirl/APCC wins** — it is five years newer and
small-animal specific. Verify every dose against a current formulary before shipping.
